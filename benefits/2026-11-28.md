# Benefits — 2026-11-28

How we can use today's updates and apps for our benefit. No new releases today — the value is in the apps and the two warnings.

## Test v16.61.0 printing on staging before any client rollout

- **What:** Print to PDF is reported broken after upgrading to ERPNext v16.61.0 (button spins, selected print format ignored).
- **Benefit for us:** Turn it into a checklist item — every v16.61.0 upgrade now gets a print-format test on staging first. That is a concrete quality step NexTash can show clients, and it prevents an embarrassing "invoices won't print" call the day after an upgrade. Keep watching the forum thread for the fix.

## Warn India clients about the E-Way Bill change before 1 January 2027

- **What:** E-Way Bill numbers go from 12 to 14 digits on 1 January 2027; India Compliance already handles it except for the "Mark as Generated" dialog, where a fix is in progress.
- **Benefit for us:** A short, proactive message to India clients ("we're tracking this, one dialog fix is coming") is exactly the kind of advisory that keeps a client. When the India Compliance update lands, it is a billable mini-task: apply it and verify a 14-digit e-Way Bill end to end.

## Use the CVE as a reason to upgrade old-version clients

- **What:** CVE-2026-134426 — missing authorization in an ERPNext Accounts endpoint, affecting versions before 15.121.4 / 16.36.0.
- **Benefit for us:** Any client still on an old v15 or early v16 is exposed. That is a factual, non-salesy reason to propose their upgrade now — and an upgrade project is billable work that also removes a real risk.

## Offer a coffee-chain / restaurant POS package

- **What:** Two complete POS apps landed in one day — mypos (coffee chains, on native POS Invoices, tested on 16.61.0) and servepos (restaurants, with desktop POS and an Android waiter app).
- **Benefit for us:** Cafes, juice bars and restaurants are everywhere in Lahore and across Pakistan, and POS is usually the first system they buy. NexTash can now demo a working POS on ERPNext instead of building one from scratch — mypos for counter-driven coffee/drink shops, servepos where tables and a waiter app matter. The Vietnam payment methods in mypos need swapping for local QR/bank payments, so scope that as part of the implementation, not a blocker.

## Offer QuickBooks-to-ERPNext migration with verified history — and AI bookkeeping on top

- **What:** erpnext-agent-tools migrates a QuickBooks Online company to ERPNext with full, verified history, then lets AI agents work the books read-only by default, writing only through human-approved proposals.
- **Benefit for us:** Migration from QuickBooks is a service NexTash already sells; a tool that proves the migration against QuickBooks' own numbers makes the sales conversation much easier ("we verify every balance"). The AI-agent layer is a second, newer offer: "AI bookkeeper, with a human approving everything" — a service line competitors are not offering yet. Try the migration CLI on a test QuickBooks sandbox first and record the result.

## Offer ZKTeco attendance as a standard HRMS add-on

- **What:** washingtonia_attendance is a tested ZKTeco ADMS gateway that pushes punches straight into HRMS Employee Checkin records.
- **Benefit for us:** Almost every office and factory client in Pakistan has ZKTeco devices, and attendance sync is asked for in nearly every HRMS implementation. Having a tested gateway ready turns a custom-integration quote into a standard add-on — faster to sell, faster to deliver. Pilot it against a real ZKTeco terminal on a staging bench before offering it.

## Pilot WhatsApp messaging from ERPNext

- **What:** whatsapp_connector sends WhatsApp messages from ERPNext (order updates, invoices, reminders) via a desktop bridge.
- **Benefit for us:** WhatsApp is the channel Pakistani customers actually read. A working "your order is ready / your invoice is due" flow from ERPNext is an easy upsell for retail and distribution clients. Needs the companion desktop bridge and a staging pilot first — do not promise it to a client until the pilot works.

## Use the training quiz app for client onboarding

- **What:** erpnext_evaluation is a quiz/assessment app for ERPNext training (MCQ, True/False, open-ended, batches, trainee portal, auto-scoring).
- **Benefit for us:** Client training is part of every implementation — a built-in quiz turns "we trained your staff" into something measurable ("your team scored X on the Accounts module"). It also fits the research hub's learning material. Pilot it with one training batch.

## Evening update — 21:03 PKT

### Offer contract management as a gap-filler for clients drowning in spreadsheets

- **What:** contract_management is a Contract Lifecycle Management app with a counterparty portal, MIT licensed, with CI.
- **Benefit for us:** Most clients track supplier and customer contracts in spreadsheets and lose renewals. A CLM app inside ERPNext is a small, sellable add-on to any implementation — and the counterparty portal gives the client something to show their own customers and suppliers. Pilot it on staging first; the README is thin, so confirm the feature set before quoting it.

### Offer parcel/freight tracking to courier and fulfilment clients — after checking the original

- **What:** cargoops is a parcel/freight operations app with EasyPost and 17Track tracking, but it is a renamed copy of AgileShift/cargo_management.
- **Benefit for us:** Courier and e-commerce fulfilment clients in Pakistan need tracking inside ERPNext, and this is the most complete option seen. Before offering it, compare it with the original AgileShift app and pilot the tracking keys on staging — a re-publish needs that extra check, and doing it is exactly the diligence clients pay NexTash for.

### Sell spend-approval control to finance teams

- **What:** LedgerGate blocks Purchase Orders above a company limit until a manager approves them, with a full decision log.
- **Benefit for us:** Finance teams ask for spend control constantly, and a server-side block is much stronger than a policy document. The interactive demo makes it easy to show a client in five minutes. Check the missing license first — do not install an unlicensed app on a client site.

### Pilot Telegram alerts for order and payment updates

- **What:** erpnext_telegram sends Telegram (and SMS) notifications from ERPNext to customers, suppliers and staff.
- **Benefit for us:** Pakistani customers read Telegram/WhatsApp, not email. "Your order is dispatched / your payment is due" alerts from ERPNext are a cheap, visible win for retail and distribution clients. Pilot it on staging, and compare with the original yrestom app it is forked from.

### Track AI project costs — for our own AI work too

- **What:** ai_cost_manager tracks GPU costs per project: margin, budget, usage-based billing. MIT, French README.
- **Benefit for us:** Niche, but directly relevant as NexTash does more AI work — knowing what each project's GPU usage costs is the difference between a profitable AI service and a loss-making one. Worth a look for internal use first, before offering it to AI-team clients.

### Watch the Handling Units design — it solves a real manufacturing ask

- **What:** A community design draft adds Handling Units (pallets/cartons, GS1 SSCC) to ERPNext v16 via Inventory Dimensions.
- **Benefit for us:** Manufacturing and warehouse clients ask for pallet-level tracking, and there is no good answer in stock ERPNext today. Following this thread (and issue #55521) keeps NexTash ready with the answer when a client asks — and contributing our own experience there is cheap visibility in the community.
