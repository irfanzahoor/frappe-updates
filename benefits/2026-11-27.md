How we can use today's updates and apps for our benefit. Today was quiet on releases, but one new app opens a real vertical — plus two recent releases still worth acting on.

## Fossible HIS (Biograph) — a hospital vertical for NexTash

- **Offer hospital implementations:** clinics and small hospitals in Pakistan run on scattered systems (a billing tool, a lab tool, paper registers). Fossible HIS puts OPD/IPD, lab, radiology, pharmacy, billing/insurance and HR on one ERPNext site. This is a new service line NexTash can pitch — a demo site on Frappe Cloud (one-click Biograph install) is the fastest way to show it.
- **Use it as the clinical layer, sell the ERP around it:** the app is a fork of Marley Healthcare, so the ERPNext Healthcare module, accounting, inventory and HRMS knowledge NexTash already has transfers directly. The implementation work — chart of accounts, pharmacy stock, billing/RCM setup, user training — is the billable part.
- **Lead with the FHIR / integrations angle:** FHIR R4, HL7 and the Hydra MCP agent surface mean lab machines, insurance panels and AI agents can plug in. For a hospital group or diagnostic lab chain, that integration story is the differentiator against a proprietary HIMS.
- **Be honest about maturity:** the codebase is a fork with 2 stars and a recent rename (biograph → HealthCentral). Pilot it on a staging bench, check the Healthcare doctypes against the client's actual workflows, and do not promise go-live dates until the pilot passes.

## Evening update — 21:03 PKT

### HRMS v16.60.1 — small payroll and attendance fixes worth taking

- **Stop stray payslip emails:** when a Salary Slip is cancelled or deleted, its unsent emails are now removed from the Email Queue. If a client ever voided a slip and the employee still got the email, this is the fix — apply it on the next staging pass and mention it to payroll clients as a quiet reliability win.
- **Expense claims against advances are now correct:** the unclaimed amount no longer gets reduced by other employees' claims paid through the same Journal Entry. For clients who pay advances and settle them through shared journal entries, this removes a real source of wrong balances — worth a quick check on any client site that uses Employee Advances heavily.
- **Attendance check-ins sync to the right shift end:** automatic check-in syncing now uses the most recent shift end that has passed. Clients with shift workers who saw delayed or missed check-ins should see that clear up after this patch.
- **RTL clients get a correct org chart:** the Organizational Chart and directory now mirror properly in right-to-left languages — a small but visible fix for Arabic/Urdu-speaking clients.
