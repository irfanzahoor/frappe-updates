### Print to PDF breaks after upgrading to ERPNext v16.61.0 (forum, 27–28 Nov)

- **What:** A user on Frappe Cloud upgraded to ERPNext 16.61.0 and reports the Print to PDF button just keeps spinning without printing, and the PRINT button ignores the selected print format. If the Print Format is left blank, both PDF and print work — but then the document has no proper format. No fix or workaround yet; the thread is still open.
- **Source:** https://discuss.frappe.io/t/print-to-pdf-error-after-updating-to-erpnext-16-61-0/166042
- **What it means:** v16.61.0 changed something in document printing (v16.60 also shipped the new report printing experience). Before rolling v16.61.0 out to a client, test Print to PDF with their actual print formats on staging — especially invoices and quotations. If a client already upgraded and hits this, the temporary fallback is a blank print format, and the thread is the place to watch for a fix.

### E-Way Bill numbers change from 1 January 2027 — India Compliance is nearly ready (forum, 27 Nov)

- **What:** A GSTN advisory dated 4 November 2026 changes E-Way Bill numbers from 12 to 14 digits and Consolidated E-Way Bill numbers from 10 to 11 digits, effective 1 January 2027. The India Compliance team replied on the forum: the app already handles 14-digit e-Way Bill numbers almost everywhere (generating, updating, cancelling, fetching, printing — stored and sent as-is, with no fixed length). The one exception is the "Mark as Generated" dialog, where a manually entered e-Way Bill is still validated as 12 digits only — a fix to accept both 12 and 14 digits is in progress. Consolidated e-Way Bills are not generated through India Compliance, so the 10 → 11 change does not affect ERPNext users.
- **Source:** https://discuss.frappe.io/t/e-way-bill-numbering-format-changes-from-1-january-2027/166031
- **What it means:** For clients in India, nothing to do right now — but watch for the India Compliance update that fixes the "Mark as Generated" dialog before 1 January 2027, and flag it to any client who enters e-Way Bills manually.

### Security note: CVE-2026-134426 — missing authorization in ERPNext Accounts (fixed in 15.121.4 / 16.36.0)

- **What:** A missing-authorization vulnerability in an ERPNext Accounts module endpoint that returns Sales Order and Purchase Order values and counterparties without checking permissions on the requested records. Affects ERPNext before 15.121.4 and before 16.36.0 (GHSA-3f93-crcf-9p9j, CVSS 4.0: 5.3 Medium). Published 21 November, updated 26 November.
- **Source:** https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/security/advisories/GHSA-3f93-crcf-9p9j
- **What it means:** Current v16.61.0 / v15.127.0 sites are already past the fixed versions, so nothing to do there. But if any client is still on an older v15 (below 15.121.4) or v16 (below 16.36.0), that site is exposed — use this as a reason to get them upgraded.

### Community note: Ukrainian localization suite moves to v16 (forum, 27 Nov)

- **What:** The UA Pro ERP team in Kyiv, who maintain a 59-module Ukrainian localization suite on ERPNext (tax invoices, Nova Poshta / Ukrposhta shipping, Checkbox fiscal receipts, bank import, SAF-T UA export, marketplace connectors), have migrated everything to Frappe / ERPNext v16 and shared what they learned — including a v16 gotcha: custom stock reports can no longer read `tabStock Ledger Entry.batch_no` directly, because batch quantities now live inside Serial and Batch Bundle.
- **Source:** https://discuss.frappe.io/t/ukrainian-localization-for-erpnext-tax-invoices-nova-poshta-checkbox-fiscal-receipts-bank-import/164944 · overview: https://ua-pro.net
- **What it means:** A useful real-world v16 migration lesson — if a client's custom stock or manufacturing reports read batch numbers from the Stock Ledger, they need to join Serial and Batch Bundle after a v16 upgrade. Also a reminder of how much a full country localization takes (59 modules), which is worth knowing when scoping localization work.

### Handling Units (license plates) — a community design draft for pallet and carton tracking on v16 (forum, 27 Nov)

- **What:** A community member working on a manufacturing project with batches and medical devices is building a Frappe app that adds Handling Units (license plates, SAP-style) to ERPNext v16, because v16 has no object for tracking pallets and cartons as stock. The design is built on v16 Inventory Dimensions: the Handling Unit is a custom dimension on the Stock Ledger Entry (mandatory flag, negative-stock check per dimension), so stock per HU stays in the standard stock ledger with no parallel stock to sync. Planned features: Handling Unit doctype with GS1 SSCC, packaging type, status and location; several items and batches in one HU; nesting (cartons inside a pallet); pack / unpack / repack as standard Stock Entries; moving a whole HU with one scan; "HU-managed" warehouses where the dimension is mandatory; packing instructions per item or customer; links to Delivery Note, Packing Slip and Shipment; SSCC labels. The author is asking for comments, prior art, and collaborators/testers. Related GitHub issue: frappe/erpnext #55521.
- **Source:** https://discuss.frappe.io/t/handling-units-license-plates-on-erpnext-v16-a-design-draft-built-on-inventory-dimensions/166052
- **What it means:** No app published yet — a design to watch, not install. But pallet/carton tracking is a real ask from manufacturing and warehouse clients, and this Inventory Dimensions approach is the cleanest shape seen so far. If a client needs it, this thread (and issue #55521) is where the solution is forming — and a good place to contribute experience.
