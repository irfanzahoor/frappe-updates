## Evening update — 21:03 PKT

### Frappe HRMS v16.60.1 — bug-fix patch (published 27 Nov, 15:48 PKT)

- **What:** A small patch release — all bug fixes, no new features. [Release notes](https://github.com/frappe/hrms/releases/tag/v16.60.1)
  - **Payroll:** cancelling or deleting a Salary Slip now removes its unsent emails from the Email Queue (already-sent emails are kept) — no more stray payslip emails going out after a slip is voided.
  - **Expenses:** the unclaimed amount on an Expense Claim is now calculated from approved, submitted claims against the same Employee Advance, so claims by other employees paid through the same Journal Entry no longer reduce it.
  - **Attendance & Shifts:** automatic check-in syncing in Shift Type now uses the most recent shift end that has already passed, preventing attendance delays caused by a future or outdated shift end.
  - **General:** the Organizational Chart, directory, popovers and downloaded chart image are now mirrored for right-to-left languages; primary buttons in Payroll Entry, Employee Referral and Employee Tax Exemption Declaration keep readable labels on hover.
- **Also released:** [HRMS v15.64.10](https://github.com/frappe/hrms/releases/tag/v15.64.10) (15:24 PKT) — the same Email Queue and Shift Type fixes backported to v15, plus a fix that uses the latest submitted Salary Structure Assignment (ignoring draft/cancelled ones) to determine an employee's currency.

### What it means for you

- **Low-risk patch, safe to apply:** five small fixes, no schema or behaviour changes to plan around. Worth taking on the next staging pass, especially for clients running payroll — the Salary Slip email fix and the Expense Claim calculation fix both stop real mistakes (wrong emails, wrong unclaimed amounts).
- **HRMS is now the newest release:** v16.60.1 (27 Nov) overtakes ERPNext v16.61.0 (25 Nov) as the latest release across the apps. Everything else is unchanged since the morning: ERPNext v16.61.0, Framework v16.61.0, CRM v16.5.0, Helpdesk v16.2.2, Insights v3.51.0, Raven v3.2.0.
